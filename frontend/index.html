Supported classes

The model is trained to recognize four tomato leaf conditions.

Healthy

No visual signs of blight, mold, or lesions detected on the leaf surface.

Early Blight

Characterized by concentric dark spots that typically appear on older leaves first.

Late Blight

Marked by irregular water-soaked patches that can spread quickly across the leaf.

Leaf Mold

Identified by pale patches on the upper leaf and olive-green mold beneath.
